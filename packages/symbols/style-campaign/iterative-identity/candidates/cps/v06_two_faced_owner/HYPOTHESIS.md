An owned pair has two useful faces: names first and ids first. A sum of consumers selects exactly one orientation, so the owner can expose either store first without copying either store. `Names{v => ...}` and `Ids{m => ...}` give the public operations one common fold rhythm and let response continuations consume the other store. The join operation expresses the only swap, visibly and symmetrically. No key/polarity or result-conversion algebra is added.
