# Author reading judgment before inspecting after-ratings

The first candidate checks without a diagnostic retry. It removes two duplicated
terminal clauses without adding a helper, datatype, argument or name. The first
two wildcard-fuel clauses visibly say that completion and failure are independent
of remaining fuel. The two Searching clauses then distinguish exhaustion from
progress. The recursive call and halving arithmetic are byte-identical.

I prefer this four-clause grouping to the six-clause product enumeration. The
benefit is the visible separation of terminal versus advancing states, not its
line count. The tradeoff is that a reader must know that `_` ignores either Nat
constructor; the baseline spells each combination out. Both remain exact and
locally traceable. No complexity has moved to a helper. This is the author's
preference, not a measured user preference or an automatic three-axis pass.

Provisional disposition: accept as an unreleased structural improvement only if
all frozen gates pass and scaling shows no material regression. Keep low or
uncertain style dimensions visible rather than declaring campaign completion.
The provider command returned attention status, but its after-distributions have
not yet been inspected at the time of this judgment.
