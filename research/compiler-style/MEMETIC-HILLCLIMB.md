# Hill-climbing Knot's memetic power

Plan requested 2026-09-26. Compiler Planning owns this plan and its
[planning state](hillclimb/state.json). Execution has not started. The
[theory research](MEMETIC-THEORY.md) supplies hypotheses; the
[reading specimen](MEMETIC-PRIMER.md) supplies initial material.

The objective is a recognizable way of writing and thinking that a technically
fluent reader wants to learn, can extend, and wants to return to. Develop that
identity before resuming compiler implementation. Keep the full self-hosting,
Wasm and generated-GPU goal, existing contracts and independent gates intact.

## Search over families, retain individual accountability

One search unit is a bounded family: its actual Bend, essential datatypes, one
central law, two uses and a short explanation. Its form must remain interesting
when the explanation and project name are removed. Source, naming, composition,
visual rhythm and teaching sequence are separate dimensions we can vary.

Keep three objects distinct:

- **Production baseline:** the committed implementation and its known limitations.
  It stays in place until a qualified replacement is integrated by its owner.
- **Search lead:** the strongest current specimen under a stated reading
  hypothesis. A correct but below-target specimen may lead further experiments
  in isolation; it is not an accepted implementation or a passing pilot.
- **Alternative:** a materially different promising approach retained so that
  local improvement does not eliminate a better direction too early.

Keep at most three live approaches. Preserve every attempted candidate and its
diagnostics in the archive. Improvements in a family do not average away weak
declarations: every changed or introduced declaration retains its own three-axis
rating and current acceptance bar.

## What we optimize and what stays fixed

| Evidence channel | Concrete question | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attraction | Which version would you choose to read or write more of, and why? | Comparison, reader's exact reason, tie or uncertainty |
| Recognition | Without its label, can the reader recognize the form in another example? | Choice, remembered motif, confusion with the generic control |
| Reconstruction | After the example is removed, what can the reader reproduce? | Actual expression, lost distinctions, required hints |
| Transfer | Can the reader use the grammar for a new operation with a complete contract? | Actual implementation, semantic gates, extra vocabulary and exceptions |
| Existing style criteria | Does every changed declaration meet each of the three targets? | Full distributions, context limits and source/model/rubric identities |

These form an evidence vector, not a weighted virality score. Author judgment
screens candidates. Fresh model contexts can diagnose confusion and attempt
transfer. Neither is a human adoption result. A user comparison of concrete
examples anchors taste; additional readers, if available, strengthen the account
without being invented or silently treated as independent repetitions.

Fix semantics, public behavior, quantity/lifetime rules, laws, independent model,
expected observations, semantic mutants, backend boundaries and applicable cost
limits before generation. Preserve error precedence and deliberate asymmetry.
Freeze rubric, context policy and reviewer model within an epoch. A new model or
rubric starts a separately labeled comparison series.

The project threshold remains probability mass at levels 3/4 of at least 60% on
**each** axis for **each** changed/introduced declaration. It is not a probability
of human preference. Small movements in those masses are weak evidence and do
not, by themselves, replace the search lead.

## Sequence

| Phase | Work | Exit artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Freeze and align | Reconcile current pilot evidence; select the family, gates, controls, mutation budget and transfer contract | Exact baseline and trial preregistration |
| 1. Find a promising direction | Compare three distinct treatments of the same problem before polishing any one | One search lead and up to two alternatives, with reasons |
| 2. Climb locally | Make focused changes, compare with the parent, and retain only demonstrated gains | Candidate lineage and explicit keep/archive/reject decisions |
| 3. Test transfer | Remove explanatory framing; reconstruct and solve fresh operations | Transfer artifacts and fixed-gate results |
| 4. Establish the style reference | Extract the successful vocabulary and expression grammar; test a fresh example | Small executable primer and conventions grounded in examples |
| 5. Propagate by owner | Apply the grammar to another family, then another component | Qualified owner commits and an updated inventory of remaining debt |

### Phase 0: fix the baseline and the experiment

The current runner candidate is another owner's staged work. Its completed
[retained receipt](memetic-theory-evidence-2026-09-26.json) gives 82% conceptual,
81% delight and 52% memetic: **uncertain, not success**. The shared campaign state
still describes that rating as unavailable. Its owner must reconcile the records;
do not rerun the same source merely to obtain a different score.

The existing pilot allows one substantive candidate with a bounded diagnostic
retry. The search budget below is a **proposed successor protocol**, not extra
attempts retroactively attached to that pilot. Before execution, record its
separate trial identities, scope and budget in the owner-controlled campaign
state. Retain the old result unchanged. The wider queue stays held until the
required scoped feasibility result is actually achieved. This planning turn

Start with the owned task family: `run`, its `Run` representation and the directly

Existing step/frame behavior and independent observations stay frozen.

Prepare four reference items: the committed baseline, the current candidate,
a names-only treatment, and an intentionally over-styled treatment. The latter

semantic content and explanatory context; do not make one version look stronger

Reserve a transfer rendering before generation. A suitable initial contract is
an owned runner driven by a list of Nat budgets: empty budgets preserve state;

### Phase 1: explore three directions on the same problem

1. **Held future:** make the owned remainder of computation tangible.
2. **Conservation:** make accepted and refused handoffs visibly account for owners.
3. **Lawful transformation:** make an operation and its composition law reveal
   the same structure.

These are starting hypotheses, not mandatory project terminology. Require each
treatment to name a concrete source-level mechanism: a state representation,
paired outcomes, a composition rule or a recurring case grammar. A new slogan
around unchanged code counts only as a framing experiment.

Record the author's expected gain before review. Present viable alternatives
with neutral labels and comparable context. The first useful user taste request
should show actual code, take one short comparison, and ask which version creates
an appetite for more. Do not ask the user to design our rubric or approve every
iteration. If no response is available, continue preparing and screening examples
while leaving human preference unobserved.

### Phase 2: a bounded, repeatable hill-climbing loop

The proposed first epoch has **three rounds of at most three substantive
candidates: nine total**. This is a resource limit, not a statistical guarantee.
Each candidate has one recorded initial generation and at most one separately
recorded compiler-diagnostic retry. Use Astra at max reasoning as required by the
campaign. A further substantive edit receives a new candidate ID and consumes
another slot. Never conceal repair attempts or replace earlier evidence.

- **Round 1:** the three distinct directions above.
- **Round 2:** up to two focused neighbors of the lead and one challenge from
  another direction.
- **Round 3:** up to two improvements informed by reconstruction/transfer failures
  and one deliberately different representation. Reuse earlier results when
  source, context and review identities match.

Change one principal mechanism per candidate. Useful moves include replacing
tuple plumbing with a meaningful state, exposing a dual operation, factoring one
real invariant, making an ownership outcome explicit, or composing existing
operators. Naming, layout and explanation can then clarify that mechanism.
Record incidental changes so the claimed cause stays assessable.

For each candidate:

1. Freeze parent, source scope, hypothesis, predicted payoff and failure condition.
2. Generate in an isolated candidate copy; preserve source and first diagnostics.
3. Run syntax/type/quantity/proof checks and the fixed applicable independent
   behavior, mutation, backend and cost gates. Reject semantic failures before
   paying for aesthetic review.
4. Inspect the source against the parent and controls. Record concrete reading
   evidence before seeing style scores. A weaker idea with a catchy name is a
   framing result, not a source improvement.
5. Run targeted semantic Perch and all three style ratings once per matching
   candidate identity. Keep missing coverage, unresolved context, stale inputs
   and provider failures explicit. No whole-project paid scan for a local trial.
6. Attempt the frozen reconstruction/transfer exercise on the strongest viable
   forms, preserving prompts, contexts, assistance and actual outputs.
7. Record disposition and the next move. Keep code, prose and source status
   separate; a useful teaching edit need not justify an implementation rewrite.

Replace the search lead only with a concrete benefit in reading or transfer,
supported by the actual diff, without an observed semantic or applicable cost
regression. When attraction and transfer disagree, retain both candidates in the
small archive and run the exercise that distinguishes them. A model-score rise
alone is insufficient. Below-target leads remain isolated research.

Integrate only after fixed gates, a concrete reading gain, a successful transfer
exercise and all required declaration targets pass. Record whether the reading
evidence is author, model or human. Family praise cannot waive a failed member.

### Phase 3: try to break the apparent gain

For a finalist, remove the primer's enthusiastic explanation and project label.
Show equal context for parent and candidate. Preserve essential convention
definitions; removing the language needed to read the code would test something
else. Reverse presentation order across comparisons when possible and keep model
scores hidden from readers until their responses are recorded.

Then use two transfer distances: an adjacent task operation, followed by a
different semantic family such as owned-slot transfer or compiler binding
refinement. Teach the grammar first, then supply complete requirements. Record
where it needs extra terms, escapes or a different idea. Successful reuse may
share a visual/compositional principle without forcing every component into a
task metaphor.

Once an exercise has influenced a revision it belongs to the development set.
Reserve a new rendering for the next validation. A reader who already saw the
answer does not supply fresh reconstruction evidence. Model-only trials are
feasibility evidence; a small human sample guides local taste, not population
claims about virality.

### Phases 4–5: make the improvement compound

Build a short executable style reference from the accepted examples: the central
idea, a few expressions, their laws, the minimal vocabulary and one instructive
counterexample. Each convention must explain more than one example. Include
small supporting declarations so the identity survives ordinary code, not just
showpiece dispatchers.

Have the owning chat apply it to one further family with fixed independent gates.
Preserve each component's actual concepts; do not rename the whole repository
into one metaphor. Integrate by explicit owner commits. Update the primer,
whiteboard and relevant documentation from demonstrated source, then refresh the
inventory without paid calls. Measure paid coverage at wave boundaries only when
it adds new evidence. Keep the existing maximum of two active package/research
batches and 24 reviewed declarations per batch after broader dispatch is allowed.

The Symbols owner currently records a separately authorized iterative search in
its local workspace. Treat that as owner-local work to inspect and learn from;
do not duplicate it, assume its authority applies everywhere, or count its
progress as validation of this plan. Published package versions and dependency
pins remain under their existing release procedure.

## Plateau policy and exit criteria

After two rounds without a defensible gain, stop making cosmetic neighbors.
Classify the failure: weak central idea, unsuitable representation, missing
context, judge/human disagreement, syntax friction or loss of semantic economy.
Use the next available candidate for a different mechanism. If the nine-candidate
epoch ends without a winner, retain the baseline and publish the failed hypotheses;
start a newly bounded epoch only with a changed explanation of the failure.

If human-preferred, semantically sound examples systematically fail the judge,
run a separate context/calibration investigation with fresh examples. Version any
resulting policy change. Do not lower a threshold mid-epoch or relabel old failures.
If the judge likes a form that readers cannot reconstruct or do not want, improve
the form. These are different diagnoses.

The recommended foundation exit is one qualified family, a concrete user taste
anchor, and successful adjacent and cross-family transfer with independent gates.
Its compact conventions and examples should be sufficient to guide the next
compiler representation decision. Until then, keep compiler implementation behind
this priority. Completing a document, exhausting an epoch or obtaining one good
score does not complete the foundation or the larger compiler goal.

Each epoch report fits on one review surface: parent and best candidate, the
reading hypothesis, exact changed scope, all three distributions, fixed-gate
results, transfer output, explicit disposition, cost/attempt totals and the next
uncertainty. Commit coherent owned work; preserve other chats' staged files.
