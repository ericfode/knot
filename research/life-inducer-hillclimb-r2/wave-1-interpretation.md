# First-wave interpretation, recorded before held-out validation

The user requested the lessons before the next wave. Wave 2 had already started;
these observations were communicated while it was running, before wave 3.
They do not modify the frozen selection rule, author feedback or acceptance bar.

All ten first-wave trajectories produced a behavior-passing implementation.
Twenty-nine of their 30 rounds passed behavior; one exhausted its compiler repair.
No trajectory passed the complete current Perch policy.

| GPT-6 Astra, low | 5 | 0 | 0.3700 | 167.2 seconds |
| GPT-5.6 Sol, xhigh | 5 | 0 | 0.5748 | 545.6 seconds |

Elapsed time includes author calls, compiler repairs and gates. These are small,
adaptive-search samples; partial-score means are not probabilities of passing.
All author identities are requested settings, not server-reported resolution.

The dominant observed obstacle is the conditional Galaxy-brain target. All 173
declaration visits in the 29 style-reviewed rounds were classified critical.
The only two passing big-brain assessments were datatypes, which retain target
level 3. No reviewed function met its required level-5 target. T004 round 3 met
all delight, memetic, anticipation and payoff assessments across five functions,
while all five functions missed Galaxy brain. Repeated declaration visits are
dependent observations, not 173 independent specimens.

There are concrete source-level ideas: packed-row bitplanes in T006 and a compact
Life polynomial in T004. These are more substantial than renaming alone.
[Editorial observations](reading-observations.md) distinguish saved model
judgments from direct code reading. No candidate runtime benchmark has been run.

The [first selection](wave-1-selection.json) chose `w1-n1`, a 4,793-byte mutation
of the 9,532-byte original. Its two best eligible search rounds averaged 0.6167
of required assessments met, versus 0.4583 for the original pair. This selected
comparison cannot establish an inducer effect. Fresh original, selected and
no-inducer held-out trajectories remain necessary. The historical six-round v2
success is not comparable to this stricter v3 policy or these author settings.

Disposition: promising code representations and an observed author-time tradeoff;
no reliable inducer clearing the complete current bar. Continue the preregistered
bounded search and report the held-out results separately.
