# Parent reading before Perch

The visible mechanism is separable neighborhood summation: halo computes a
three-cell row sum, pulse combines three such rows and removes the center,
and sweep carries the old middle halo into the next row. This gives concrete
repetition with variation rather than decorative vocabulary. front unifies
two boundary depths. The main potential friction is pulse's four lists: the
raw row and its center halo must remain aligned, and callers need to understand
that relationship. strips and evolve are conventional boundary adapters.

This is the parent model's reading, not a human preference or an independent
blind judgment. No Perch result had been observed when this note was written.
