## Reading result

The unexposed selection is a packed-row pipeline. One duplex recurrence emits
an output bit while building the next input word through its continuation.
A bit-plane census includes the center and selects total three, or total four
with a live center. Two discarded startup rows and two drain passes align the
old-row window with public output. This is compact but the codec recurrence
remains the uncertain memetic judgment. Round 10 uses the same parity/carry
grammar on both spatial axes. The author selected that reading improvement
over round 7, whose slightly higher 0.59 memetic mass still did not pass.

The exposed selection makes boundaries structural. A dead north row, appended
dead bottom row and east sentinels provide a regular window. The pulse carries
the old west column and writes the literal eight-neighbor sum with its center
missing. No updated cell enters the next window. It meets the recorded targets,
including the narrow 0.61 memetic margin.

This is one adaptive search per condition, with different representations,
declaration counts and stopping times. The credit interruption was recovered
before the unexposed arm used its final round.
It does not establish a causal inducer benefit. Both authors also disclosed
seeing native-fixture values embedded in their own first-round gate receipts;
own feedback was allowed in this phase. They report no oracle-source access or
fixture-specific code. Reading separation is procedural, not a filesystem sandbox.

## Credit blocker and resumption

[The failed invocation](arm-a/round-09/semantic-command.json) records HTTP 402:
the TypeSafe organization has no API credits. One request received zero
responses. The original runner then tried to parse absent JSON and recorded
`substring not found`; the retained stderr identifies the actual billing cause.
No style call followed that failure; provider calls stayed stopped until the
user confirmed replenishment.

The user replenished credits. The parent verified every frozen input and the
round-9 source hash, reused its matching behavior receipt, and made new semantic
and style calls in arm-a/round-09/recovery/. These completed without findings
but left one uncertain memetic rating. The original failure was not overwritten.

The same unexposed author then completed round 10 and stopped at the budget.
The exposed author remained stopped at its first pass. The final selected-source
law packet completed all eight checks with no threshold findings. There are no
remaining authorized rounds or pending reviews; no additional round, unchanged
reroll, credit purchase or automatic polling was performed.
