# Reading judgment before after-scores

The first candidate and all twelve unchanged proofs check without a diagnostic
retry. The single progressing case is now one line: a positive budget paired
with a checkpoint becomes one step followed by the remaining run. Its complement
returns the existing state unchanged. The recurrence and identity read together,
without a special reconstruction of Delivered.

I prefer this expression of the fixed point to the separate zero-fuel and
positive-fuel Delivered arms. It makes complete-owner preservation literal and
removes an unnecessary reconstruction. The cost is that the terminal constructor
is no longer named in the match. The unchanged Run datatype and existing comment
provide that vocabulary, but the judge may reasonably prefer the explicit arm.

This is the author's reading preference. It is not a human preference supplied
by the user, a style pass, or permission to keep the candidate if the scoped
pilot's unchanged three-axis or independent acceptance gates fail. No after-score
has been inspected and no style request has been made for this candidate yet.
