# Declaration contributions and complete reader units

Disposition: **retain this draft as advisory; production v6 is unchanged.**
Separating a declaration's contribution from the whole mechanism's expressive
hook resolves several exposed failures without relabelling central recurrences
as helpers. The reserved controls leave an unresolved calibration gap: ordinary,
clear contributions can still miss Memetic level 2. No complete reader unit
meets all targets in either arm, and no historical failure becomes a pass.

## Question and fixed policy

The IntMap owner proposed different obligations for internal implementation,
proof transport and thin wrappers after the bounded style trials. This study
tests the unit of judgment, rather than public/private visibility. A declaration
inside a preselected complete reader unit gets Compression/Delight level 3 and
Memetic/Anticipation/Payoff level 2. The complete unit keeps the three expressive
axes at level 3. Every probability threshold remains 0.60. A private or
single-function mechanism still owes the complete-unit judgment.

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) and [draft policy](draft-policy.json) were frozen
before authoring the fresh controls. The [corpus](corpus.json), source,
expectations and exact requests were then frozen before paid review. The v6
axis definitions remain intact, including memetic appeal's independence from
technical value. Draft declaration requests append a contribution instruction
only to the three expressive axes. Original role distributions remain visible
as diagnostics; centrality does not change the draft's declaration obligation.

This is a supplementary full-source comparison, not a production evaluator
implementation. All rows explicitly retain `automatic_pass: false`. Unknown
scope cannot qualify. Existing Galaxy relevance and deterministic acceptance
are unchanged.

## Results

Four source groups received both policies: eight file-group checks, 62 policy
rows, 58 distinct Jev 1.13.0 requests and four exact composition-request reuses.
No retries, source edits or rubric tuning followed the results. Full normalized
distributions and original answers are in [results.json](results.json) and
[responses](responses/); compressed exact inputs are in [requests](requests/).

Counts below mean **all five numerical declaration targets met**, not automatic
qualification. The middle column changes only target levels on the same fresh
v6 answers; the draft column comes from separately rated contribution prompts.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| E01: exposed IntMap law/fill pairs | 4/8 | 8/8 | 8/8 | .24 / .13 / .45 |
| E02: exposed IntMap API and internals | 0/12 | 4/12 | 5/12 | .15 / .09 / .31 |
| H01: fresh clear fingerprint mechanism | 0/3 | 0/3 | 1/3 | .01 / .00 / .01 |
| H02: fresh obscured equivalent | 0/4 | 0/4 | 0/4 | .05 / .01 / .04 |

Composition requests are identical between arms. Reusing them is intentional:
improving declaration eligibility cannot remove the complete-unit obligation.
All four units retain at least one failed composition bar.

The main observations are:

- `edit_here` and `edit_away` remain classified **leading** in the draft, while
  meeting its contribution targets. The experiment did not obtain those results
  by changing their role to supporting. Most of E01's numerical gain is the
  arithmetic consequence of the different targets, not improved code or a
  demonstrated improvement in judging accuracy.
- E02's five qualifying numerical rows are `IntMap.set`, `IntMap.fold`,
  `IntMap.union_with`, `edit_path` and `high`. Only **3/8 documented API
  operations** meet every draft target. Public visibility does not produce an
  automatic pass.
- The fresh clear control's `step` and one-line `Fingerprint.of` wrapper have
  draft Memetic-2 mass **.43 and .49**, both uncertain. Compression/Delight are
  .85/.76 and .81/.86. The desired treatment of ordinary, coherent contributions
  is therefore unresolved even with an explicit complete reader unit.
- The obscured `step` gets Memetic-2 mass **.63**, while Compression/Delight are
  **.11/.08**. It still fails the conjunction. This preserves the v6 distinction:
  a recognizable form can receive memetic credit without technical merit.
- `set_get` and `remove_get` are supporting in both fresh arms. Their historical
  role mismatch does not reproduce in this fuller packet. The old receipt is
  preserved; these observations neither prove a classifier defect nor establish
  that added context caused the difference.

Role uncertainty also remains explicit. In the draft it affects
`remove_preserves`, `IntMap.get`, `IntMap.remove`, and both controls' `fold`.
E01's 8/8 numerical result is not a claim that all role ambiguity disappeared.

The separate [sensitivity view](sensitivity.json) also retargets the historical
IntMap composition receipt: **3/8 becomes 4/8** using its unchanged answers.
That arithmetic is not a fresh rating or historical requalification. The fresh
matched packet's v6 4/8 is a different observation from the historical 3/8.

## Deterministic evidence and limits

Both fresh controls passed the pinned compiler and matched seven independently
specified literal results on native and JavaScript/Bun: **28 observations**.
The cases include the empty list, ordinary sequences and U32 wraparound. Exact
commands, source hashes and outputs are in [control-gates.json](control-gates.json).
These finite observations do not establish universal equivalence.

The existing `packages/int_map/PROOF.bend` and
`packages/int_map/locality/PROOF.bend` were checked again with the main checkout's
`scripts/bend-reference`; both reported `All terms check.` The existing v6
vacuity experiment's offline verifier also passed unchanged. Its six fresh
requests belong to that earlier experiment, not this run.

The new offline verifier confirms all 62 rows, exact request and response
identities, frozen source/policy identities, the scope guards and **194 unchanged
tracked IntMap/configuration files**. Production evaluator, runtime, laws,
proofs, tests, release evidence and historical receipts were not edited.

E01 uses the owner's compiler-checked mechanical combined law/fill view. The
canonical imported-template observer coverage gap remains unresolved; this
packet does not repair it or establish canonical style coverage. All full
source groups are bounded mechanism contexts, not the entire repository.

The fresh controls were reserved before scoring, but their expected reading
quality is an assistant hypothesis, not independent human taste gold. There is
one observation per distinct request. Context matters: the identical `fold`
and wrapper bodies score much worse for Compression/Delight beside the obscured
callee. Per-declaration scores should not be interpreted as intrinsic scores of
an isolated body. This study does not estimate judging accuracy or social
memetic success.

## Adoption gate

Keep the production default at v6 and the broader campaign parked. A successor
should specify contribution anchors for ordinary internals and thin wrappers,
plus an explicit reader-unit manifest and unavailable-context behavior. Freeze
those decisions before new independently labelled controls; the now-exposed
controls here must not become fresh validation. Preserve the 0.60 threshold,
the complete-unit level-3 obligation, v6's memetic/value independence and all
deterministic gates. No additional paid wave is part of this closeout.

The retained experiment can be checked without provider access:

```sh

node docs/perch-calibration/memetic-value-v6-2026-09-27/run.mjs verify
```

The runner deliberately refuses to overwrite frozen requests or results. Use a
new study directory for a successor; do not rerun `live` over this evidence.
