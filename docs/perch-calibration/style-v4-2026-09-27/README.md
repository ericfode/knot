# Potential profundity: version 4

Perch now separates a task's opportunity for a deep reframing from the quality
of its implementation. The task is rated before source reaches the provider.
Confidently high potential adds a Galaxy-brain requirement for the combined
selected mechanism; ordinary helpers retain their level-3 compression target.
Low potential keeps the ordinary targets. Missing or uncertain relevance leaves
Galaxy brain advisory, following the user's explicit preference to under-constrain
it. An advisory result does not claim that Galaxy brain was achieved.

The five task-only ratings below used the exact production request constructor,
with actual Jev 1.13.0 responses. Expectations were recorded before any calls.
No source, filenames, expected labels, previous scores or inducer entered the
requests. The rubric and thresholds were not tuned or rerolled.

| Task | Split | Mass at potential levels 3–4 | Classification |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Fixed-width hexadecimal conversion | Development | 0% | Low |
| Labelled paths across reachability, counts and costs | Development | 61% | High |
| Row-local import validation | Held out | 0% | Low |
| Incremental multiset queries and changes | Held out | 60% | High |
| Bounded Conway Life implementation | Open hypothesis probe | 0% | Low |

Both development and held-out pairs meet the pre-recorded absolute expectations.
The two high examples are near the threshold; this small assistant-defined set
does not establish agreement with human taste. Life's distribution is 36% level
0, 47% level 1 and 17% level 2. It supports the user's hypothesis that this
particular stepping contract does not warrant mandatory Galaxy brain, without
making a claim about the deeper mathematical potential of Life generally.

[development](development.json), [held out](held_out.json), and
[Life probe](hypothesis_probe.json) retain exact distributions, request/response

All 48 focused offline tests pass, including historical v3 behavior, source-blind

requests each returned the pinned model. [Test output](offline-tests.txt) is
